Daily Route

A C-shaped loop: east coast beaches → Baoting rainforest hot springs → Wuzhishan villages and terraces → Baisha tea country → west-line return.

Haikou
DAY 1
Qilou Old Street
City stroll · Arrival day
  • Stroll along Zhongshan Road and the historic Qilou arcade streets in the afternoon.
  • Dinner: Hainan rice noodles and qingbuliang dessert, keeping the first day gentle.
  • Take a second walk after dinner when the old street lights come on.
Park near Jiefang Road and walk into the old street; it is easier than driving inside.
DriveNone, city day in Haikou
StayDowntown Haikou
Wanning
DAY 2
Wenchang Chicken → Riyue Bay
Lunch stop · Beach arrival
  • Leave Haikou in the morning and stop in old Wenchang for classic poached Wenchang chicken.
  • Choose a small family restaurant; ginger-scallion-garlic dipping sauce is the point.
  • Continue south after lunch and settle into Riyue Bay in the afternoon.
  • Walk the bay at sunset and watch the surfers wrap up the day.
If energy allows, Dongjiao Coconut Grove is a quick side stop after lunch.
DriveHaikou→Wenchang about 1h; Wenchang→Wanning about 1.5h
StayRiyue Bay, Wanning
Wanning
DAY 3
Tropical River SUP
Rainforest paddleboarding · Full-day theme
  • Spend the day paddleboarding on Xinglong Sun River or a nearby tropical waterway.
  • Beginner-friendly with a local guide; suitable for both adults and children.
  • Return to the hotel pool or beach in the late afternoon instead of adding a long drive.
Book a local SUP club ahead of time; wear quick-dry clothes, reef shoes, and serious sun protection.
DriveLocal Wanning area only
StayWanning
Lingshui
DAY 4
Clearwater Bay Arrival
Singing sand · Easy beach day
  • Either do a quick tide-pool stop in Riyue Bay or drive directly south.
  • Arrive at Clearwater Bay in about one hour and check in.
  • Walk barefoot on the fine quartz sand and let the child play in the calm shallows.
  • Dinner at a seaside stall: fish soup and grilled oysters.
Clearwater Bay is best enjoyed slowly; InterContinental or Raffles-style resorts work well for this stop.
DriveWanning→Lingshui about 1h
StayClearwater Bay, Lingshui
Lingshui
DAY 5
Shuangfanshi Scuba Dive
Boat entry · Reefs · Blue-water visibility
  • Head out for a scuba session at Shuangfanshi, one of Lingshui’s best-known offshore dive sites.
  • The site is known for reef rocks, cracks, fish schools, and strong visibility in good weather.
  • Certified divers can plan a deeper fun dive; beginners should go only with a qualified local instructor.
  • Non-divers can keep the day lighter with snorkeling, beach time, or a glass-bottom boat option.
Check sea conditions before booking. June to September is generally the better window, but the operator’s call comes first.
DriveClearwater Bay→dive operator or pier, usually 30-60min depending on pickup
StayClearwater Bay, Lingshui
Lingshui→Baoting
DAY 6
Monkey Island → Baoting
Cable car · Macaques · Hot springs
  • Visit Nanwan Monkey Island in the morning, entering by cable car and keeping the visit around two hours.
  • Do not bring food onto the island; the macaques are curious and quick.
  • Drive inland to the Qixianling hot spring area after lunch and check in slowly.
  • Soak in mineral hot springs in the evening.
Choose a hotel close to Qixianling so the next day stays relaxed.
DriveLingshui→Monkey Island about 35min; Monkey Island→Baoting about 1.5h
StayQixianling hot spring area, Baoting (night 1)
Baoting
DAY 7
Yanoda Rainforest
Creeks · Rainforest · Slow hot-spring night
  • Sleep in after the transfer day.
  • Enter Yanoda Rainforest after lunch for boardwalks, giant roots, vines, and lush tropical vegetation.
  • Add the creek route or glass bridge only if everyone has energy.
  • Return for a second quiet night in Baoting.
Wear non-slip shoes and bring insect repellent; a light rain shell is easier than an umbrella.
DriveBaoting hotel→Yanoda about 30-45min
StayQixianling hot spring area, Baoting (night 2)
Baoting→Wuzhishan
DAY 8
Maona Village
Village paths · Tea · Rainforest edge
  • Leave Baoting after a slow breakfast and drive about two hours into Wuzhishan.
  • Spend the afternoon in Maona Village: Li village lanes, tea gardens, and mountain views.
  • Buy Shuiman black tea directly from the source area.
  • Dinner: local pork, wild greens, and Shanlan rice.
Stay near Shuiman Township if possible; it feels much closer to the rainforest and villages than the city center.
DriveBaoting→Wuzhishan about 2h on mountain roads
StayShuiman Township, Wuzhishan (night 1)
Wuzhishan
DAY 9
Rainforest + Terraces
Nansheng River · Yahu terraces · No mountain climb
  • Walk the tropical rainforest path along Nansheng River in the morning; it is mostly riverside and easy.
  • Rest through the warmest part of the day.
  • Visit Yahu or Shuiman terraces in the afternoon, when June rice fields are fresh green.
  • Stay for changing light on the ridges before dinner.
Wuzhishan is usually several degrees cooler than the coast; pack a thin layer.
DriveShuiman area to river/terrace stops about 15-45min
StayShuiman Township, Wuzhishan (night 2)
Baisha
DAY 10
Wulilu Tea Garden
Tea picking · Hand roasting · Muxian Lake pause
  • Drive from Wuzhishan to Baisha in the morning, about 1.5 hours on mountain roads.
  • Do a hands-on tea session at Wulilu Organic Tea Garden: pick leaves and try hand roasting.
  • Spend the afternoon around Muxian Lake and the family holiday home.
  • At night, sit outside for stars if the sky is clear.
Baisha black tea and bound tea are both good gifts; keep this first Baisha night slow.
DriveWuzhishan→Baisha about 1.5h
StayMuxian Lake area, Baisha (night 1)
Baisha
DAY 11
Muxian Lake + Yinggeling
Lake time · Rainforest road · Li brocade
  • Sleep in and have tea by the lake.
  • Plan a slow local lunch: pork, Shanlan rice, and Baisha greens.
  • In the afternoon, drive the outer Yinggeling rainforest road or loop around Baisha Reservoir.
  • Stop by a Li brocade workshop or heritage point for a final handmade souvenir.
The second Baisha night is for breathing room; do not rush back to Haikou yet.
DriveWithin Baisha, usually under 30-45min
StayMuxian Lake area, Baisha (night 2)
Return
DAY 12
Baisha → Haikou → Flight
Qiongzhong lunch · Car return · Fly out
  • Drive from Baisha toward Haikou in the morning via Qiongzhong and Tunchang.
  • Stop in Qiongzhong for bamboo-tube rice if timing works.
  • Return the rental car after arriving in Haikou; add one last bowl of Baoluo noodles if there is time.
  • Fly out the same day, so departure does not consume a separate itinerary day.
Choose an afternoon or evening flight and leave a buffer for security and car return.
DriveBaisha→Haikou Meilan Airport about 3.5-4h with lunch stop
ReturnCar return · Fly out · Trip complete

Practical Notes

Read before departure

🚗
Rental Car
  • Pick up and return in Haikou
  • 7-seat SUV is the easy choice
  • Take full insurance
  • Book online ahead of time
🌦
June Weather
  • Rainy season, often short afternoon showers
  • Plan outdoor time in the morning
  • Baisha and Wuzhishan are cooler
  • Coast is usually hot and humid
🤿
Diving
  • Book with a qualified local operator
  • Check wind, waves, and visibility
  • No heavy meal before diving
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen
👧
With A Child
  • Waterproof SPF50+ sunscreen
  • Insect repellent for rainforest days
  • Non-slip shoes for trails
  • Motion-sickness medicine for mountain roads
